Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

This is placeholder legal copy prepared for the website launch. It should be reviewed and finalised by a qualified UAE legal adviser before going live.

1. Who we are

dgnova ("we", "us") operates this website and the services described on it — real estate brokerage support, car rental, vehicle import/export coordination, travel planning and general trading. Contact details are listed on our Contact page.

2. What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect the details you provide: name, email address, phone number, the service you're interested in, your message, your preferred contact method, and — where relevant — details about a specific listing, vehicle, trip or product.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the service you asked about
  • To contact you by your chosen method (phone, WhatsApp or email)
  • To keep a record of your requirements so you don't have to repeat them

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your consent.

4. Sharing

Your details are shared only with the dgnova team members handling your enquiry, and with third parties strictly where necessary to deliver a service you've requested (for example, a shipping line for a vehicle export, or a hotel for a confirmed booking).

5. Retention and your rights

We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations. You may request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website may use basic cookies and analytics to understand how visitors use it. No enquiry data is shared with advertising networks.
