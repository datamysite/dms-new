General

One company with five divisions. That matters practically: one enquiry can cover several needs, your details don't need repeating between services, and the same working standards apply everywhere.

No. Enquiries, viewings and initial consultations are free. You only pay when you decide to proceed, and pricing is always confirmed in writing first.

We're based in Dubai and work across the UAE for property, rentals and trading delivery. Vehicle exports run from Dubai to Gulf and African countries; travel packages depart from the UAE to international destinations.

Within one business day, and usually much sooner during office hours. WhatsApp tends to get the fastest replies.

Real Estate

Yes — residential and commercial, for sale and for rent. Each listing shows its purpose clearly, and you can filter by either on the Real Estate page.

Car Rental

UAE residents: Emirates ID and a valid UAE driving licence. Visitors: passport, visit visa and a valid home-country or international driving licence. Each car's page lists its exact requirements.

Yes, comprehensive insurance is included in every rental. Excess amounts and mileage allowances are listed per car so there are no surprises.

Yes — rates improve with duration, and we can restructure an ongoing rental if your plans extend.

Car Import & Export

Gulf countries (Oman, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait) and a wide range of African destinations. Confirm your specific country in the enquiry form and we'll verify the route.

We prepare all export-side documentation and coordinate with your clearing agent at the destination. Destination duties and registration follow your country's rules — we'll explain what to expect before you commit.

Yes. Give us the make, model, year range and budget, and we'll source options from the Dubai market with inspection reports before you decide.

Travel & Tourism

Airfare and hotel rates change with dates, group size and room category. Rather than publish from-prices that later change, we confirm your details and quote a real, bookable price.

Fully. Published packages are starting points — days, hotels and activities can all be adjusted.

We advise on requirements for your nationality and assist with processing for most destinations we cover.
