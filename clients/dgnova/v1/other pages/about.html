About dgnova

One company, built for how the UAE actually works

People here rarely need just one thing. They arrive and need a home, a car and a way to move goods — often in the same month. That's the company we built.

Our Story

Why five divisions under one name

dgnova started with a simple observation: the same clients kept asking the same trusted people for help across very different things — finding a flat, hiring a car, shipping a vehicle home, planning a holiday, sourcing stock. Each time, they had to start over with a new company.

So we brought those services under one roof. Not as a directory of unrelated businesses, but as one team with shared standards: confirm the requirement properly, quote in writing, and stay reachable until handover.

We're a new company, and we'd rather be honest about that than decorate this page with invented statistics. What we bring is leadership with years of hands-on experience in each of these sectors — and the accountability of putting all of it under a single name.
