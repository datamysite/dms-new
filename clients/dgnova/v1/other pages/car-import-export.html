Car Import & Export

Vehicle export from Dubai to the Gulf and Africa

Sourcing, documentation, shipping and destination coordination — managed as one process with a single point of contact.

What We Handle

From a Dubai showroom to your destination port

Dubai is one of the best places in the world to buy vehicles — new, used and specialist. We help buyers in Gulf and African countries take advantage of that market without navigating export paperwork and shipping alone.

  • Vehicle sourcing against your specification and budget
  • Pre-shipment inspection and condition reporting
  • Export documentation prepared and reviewed with you
  • Container or RoRo shipping arranged to your port
  • Coordination with your clearing agent at destination

Destination-side duties, taxes and registration are governed by your country's rules. We explain what to expect for your route before you commit — we don't promise outcomes we don't control.

The Export Process

Six steps, each confirmed before the next

01

Submit requirement

Vehicle, budget, destination and timeline via the form below.

02

Review & sourcing

We confirm feasibility, source options and share a written quote.

03

Documentation

Export certificate, invoice and ownership papers prepared and checked.

04

Shipping prep

Inspection, photos and secure loading — container or RoRo as agreed.

05

Export & logistics

Customs clearance in Dubai and sailing confirmation with tracking details.

06

Destination handover

Documents couriered and arrival coordinated with your local agent.

Gulf destinations

Oman, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain and Kuwait — short sailings with straightforward documentation.

African destinations

Regular routes to East and West African ports, with left- and right-hand-drive availability confirmed per country.

Individuals & dealers

Single family cars or repeat dealer consignments — the process is the same, the pricing scales with volume.
